一、基本概念 / 1. Basic concepts

抗原 / Antigen 必考

先想一想 / Start here

抗体为什么能识别病毒上的某些结构？

Why can an antibody recognize certain structures on a virus?

核心结论 / Key takeaway

抗原能被特异性免疫受体或抗体识别。

An antigen can be recognized by a specific immune receptor or antibody.

为什么重要 / Why it matters

识别对象的结构有助于解释免疫应答的特异性。

The structure of the recognized target helps explain the specificity of an immune response.

概念与定义 / Concept and definition

抗原性是与抗体或免疫受体特异性结合的能力。

Antigenicity is the ability to bind specifically to an antibody or immune receptor.

应用与步骤 / Application and steps

判断一个分子是否具有抗原性时，先看它能否被特异性识别。

To assess a molecule's antigenicity, first ask whether it can be specifically recognized.

易错提醒 / Common mistake

能被识别，并不一定意味着能独立诱导免疫应答。

Being recognized does not necessarily mean being able to induce an immune response independently.

章节回顾与自测 / Chapter review and check

抗原性和免疫原性有什么区别？

What is the difference between antigenicity and immunogenicity?

参考答案 / Reference answer

抗原性描述特异性结合能力；免疫原性描述诱导免疫应答的能力。

Antigenicity describes specific binding; immunogenicity describes the ability to induce an immune response.
