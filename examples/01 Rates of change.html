本章概览 / Chapter overview

先从直线的升高量与水平位移求斜率，再用同一思路计算曲线在一个区间上的平均变化率。

Find the slope of a line from its rise and run, then apply the same idea to the average rate of change over an interval.

01

直线的斜率 / Slope of a line 页1

知识点 01 / Knowledge point

升高量与水平位移 / Rise and run 必考页1

先想一想 / Start here

沿一条直线向右走两个单位，高度增加四个单位。每向右一个单位，高度增加多少？

Move two units right along a line and gain four units of height. How much height do you gain per unit to the right?

核心结论 / Key takeaway

斜率等于升高量除以水平位移。

Slope equals rise divided by run.

为什么重要 / Why it matters

斜率把不同长度的水平位移换算成同一个单位变化量，因此可以用它描述整条直线的倾斜程度。

Slope expresses changes over different runs per unit of horizontal movement, so it describes the steepness of the whole line.

概念与定义 / Concept and definition

从横坐标 $x_1$ 移到 $x_2$ 时，升高量是纵坐标之差，水平位移是横坐标之差。

When moving from $x_1$ to $x_2$, the rise is the change in vertical coordinate and the run is the change in horizontal coordinate.

$$m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$$

应用与步骤 / Application and steps

课件原例：在 $f(x)=2x+1$ 上从 $x=1$ 移到 $x=3$，函数值从 3 增加到 7。

Slide example: on $f(x)=2x+1$, the function value rises from 3 to 7 as $x$ moves from 1 to 3.

  1. 先求升高量 / Find the rise: $7-3=4$.
  2. 再求水平位移 / Find the run: $3-1=2$.
  3. 相除求斜率 / Divide to obtain the slope: $4/2=2$.
易错提醒 / Common mistake

竖直线的水平位移为零，因此斜率没有定义。

A vertical line has zero run, so its slope is undefined.

如果升高量是 6、水平位移是 3，斜率是多少？

What is the slope when the rise is 6 and the run is 3?

参考答案 / Reference answer

斜率是 $6/3=2$。

The slope is $6/3=2$.

02

区间上的变化率 / Rate of change over an interval 页2

知识点 02 / Knowledge point

平均变化率与割线 / Average rate and secant line 重点页2

先想一想 / Start here

曲线在不同位置有不同的倾斜程度。怎样用一个数表示整个区间的变化？

A curve changes steepness along its length. How can one number describe the change across an interval?

核心结论 / Key takeaway

平均变化率等于连接两个端点的割线斜率。

The average rate of change equals the slope of the secant line through the endpoints.

为什么重要 / Why it matters

固定端点后，即使中间的变化快慢不同，仍能用平均变化率概括函数值的净变化。

Once the endpoints are fixed, the average rate summarizes the net change even if the curve changes at different speeds within the interval.

概念与定义 / Concept and definition

端点是 $(a,f(a))$ 与 $(b,f(b))$。当 $a$ 与 $b$ 不同时，把函数值之差除以输入之差。

The endpoints are $(a,f(a))$ and $(b,f(b))$. For distinct inputs $a$ and $b$, divide the change in function value by the change in input.

$$\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$$

应用与步骤 / Application and steps

课件原例：$f(x)=x^2$ 在 $[1,3]$ 上的端点函数值分别为 1 和 9，所以平均变化率为 $(9-1)/(3-1)=4$。

Slide example: for $f(x)=x^2$ on $[1,3]$, the endpoint values are 1 and 9. The average rate is $(9-1)/(3-1)=4$.

易错提醒 / Common mistake

区间端点必须不同。割线经过两个端点，计算得到的是整个区间的平均值。

The interval endpoints must differ. The secant passes through both endpoints and measures an average over the interval.

对 $f(x)=x^2$，从 1 到 2 的平均变化率是多少？

What is the average rate of change of $f(x)=x^2$ from 1 to 2?

参考答案 / Reference answer

$(4-1)/(2-1)=3$。

$(4-1)/(2-1)=3$.

The average rate on [a, b] is (f(b) - f(a)) / (b - a), with a different from b.
